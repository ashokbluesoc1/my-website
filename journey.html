My Journey Into Cybersecurity

I'm building my cybersecurity knowledge from the fundamentals upward, combining theory with practical projects and hands-on learning.

Where I Started

My journey started with curiosity about computers, networks, operating systems, and how security attacks actually happen.

Instead of learning only through theory, I decided to build projects and understand the concepts behind the tools I use.

What I'm Learning

  • Networking
  • Linux
  • Windows
  • Python
  • Cybersecurity Fundamentals
  • SOC and Blue Team
  • Threat Detection
  • Incident Response
  • Digital Forensics

My Approach

I focus on understanding how things work instead of blindly memorizing commands or copying code.

I practice by building projects, analyzing problems, debugging my own code, and revisiting concepts I've already learned.

What's Next

My goal is to continue strengthening my fundamentals and gradually move deeper into areas such as detection engineering, incident response, digital forensics, web security, cloud security, and malware analysis.
